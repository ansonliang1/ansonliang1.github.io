Formula SAE · Spring 2026

Rear wing ribs and spars

I designed the internal structure of the team's rear wing, the ribs and spars, in 7075 aluminum, working with the rest of the aero subteam.

I checked the design with a linear static study in SolidWorks Simulation, with 4500 N applied in total across the structure. The largest movement anywhere in the model was 0.049 mm. Peak stress was 27.4 MPa, far below the 505 MPa yield strength of the aluminum.

A separate linear buckling study returned a first-mode load factor of 666.5 against the load applied in that study.

Peak von Mises stress under 4500 N27.4 MPa
0Yield 505 MPa

Safety factor of about 18 on yield.

MMAE 232: Design for Innovation · Fall 2026

Sustainable chair

A foam core chair held together only by interlocking tabs and slots, with no fasteners or adhesives.

I chose the concept with a Pugh chart, then ran FEA in Autodesk Inventor. It showed a stress point under the side arch of the leg, so I lowered the arch and reran the analysis. The chair held an 80 kg load and passed testing on the first attempt.

MMAE 232: Design for Innovation · Fall 2026

Counterweight trebuchet

A team project: a counterweight trebuchet built around a required 1/4" acrylic axle, with an Arduino-controlled servo trigger.

We optimized the arm and sling dimensions with a Python simulation and checked axle stress and deflection in MATLAB. The first build missed the 4:1 arm ratio, so we rebuilt it with tighter control of the dimensions.

Launch distance after the rebuild18 m+
0Target 15 m

More than 3 m past the target.

Peak bending stress in the acrylic axle19.7 MPa
0Yield 70 MPa

Safety factor of about 3.5 at the widest support span in the design.
